About

Editing built to hook attention in the first three seconds — then hold it.

I edit video for a living — cutting short-form content, paid ad creative, corporate and event videos, and motion logo intros for brands, networking organizations, and businesses that need footage turned into something people actually watch to the end.

Every edit is built around the same throughline: pacing that keeps people watching. Fast cuts and captions for shorts, a hook-first structure for ads, and clean, professional pacing for corporate and stage content — matched to the platform it’s built for.
